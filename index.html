How Netcore can support the journey

Customer signals.
Coordinated next steps.

Illustrative workflows to evaluate, not descriptions of live Great Eastern campaigns. The proposed Netcore role is to run agreed journeys using authorised data and approved rules. Product and advice-related content remains subject to Great Eastern’s own compliance review.

Journey 01

Help new policyholders start using the app.

A signal-led follow-up for customers who have a policy but have not yet registered for digital servicing. It would sit alongside, not replace, the servicing app.

Illustrative use case
Receive

Policy active, app not yet used

Take an authorised event (for example, policy issued and no app registration), match it to a permissioned customer and check eligibility for marketing follow-up.

Execute

One relevant invitation

After an agreed wait, check status and contact rules, then send approved content on eligible channels pointing to the exact step still open, such as Great ID or Singpass sign-in.

Adapt

Exit on registration

Stop pending steps once the matched registration event arrives. Validate messages already in flight.

Proposed execution ownership: Netcore would run event entry, audience checks, delivery, contact limits, exits and reporting. Identity, authentication and servicing stay in Great Eastern systems.

Measures to agreeApp registration rate for the selected cohort · time to first login · repeat-contact rate · opt-outs · workflow effort · event-to-action time

Journey 02

Keep renewal and protection nudges coordinated.

Coordinate approved, informational customer communication around policy milestones, within Great Eastern’s own content and compliance rules.

Illustrative use case
Receive

A policy milestone or inactivity signal

Use approved eligibility rules, consent status and policy-type rules to define who may receive which message.

Execute

Channel-aware follow-up

Sequence approved content across eligible channels with one frequency cap, avoiding overlapping prompts from different teams or tools.

Adapt

Stop on action

When a payment, renewal or servicing action is recorded, suppress further prompts and move to the agreed next step.

Proposed execution ownership: Netcore would own sequencing, delivery and suppression for the selected marketing journey. Premium notices, servicing and regulatory communications stay out of scope unless agreed. Offer eligibility, content approval and disclosures remain with Great Eastern.

Measures to agreeRenewal or payment completion vs a control group · messages per customer · opt-outs · complaints · journey-change effort · total cost

Journey 03

Join digital interest to the advisor relationship.

Connect an identified, consented enquiry (for example, a request to be matched with a financial representative) to follow-up that respects advisor contact.

Illustrative use case
Receive

An enquiry or product interest

Candidate signals include an enquiry form, a quote or plan-builder step, or an event registration. Which signals can be captured, identified and consented must be confirmed.

Execute

One coordinated acknowledgement

Apply consent, frequency caps and approved content, so the customer receives one clear next step rather than several.

Adapt

Stop when the advisor connects

Suppress prompts when a representative contact, appointment or opt-out is recorded.

Proposed execution ownership: Netcore would own the agreed audience, sequence, delivery and reporting. Great Eastern keeps the website, lead and customer records, representative assignment, and decisions about who may be offered what.

Measures to agreeEnquiry-to-contact time · contact rate vs control · appointments set · messages after advisor contact · opt-outs · operating effort

Boundaries & validation

Clear ownership.
Evidence before expansion.

Permissions & contact

Confirm identity matching, consent, eligible channels and suppression rules for each journey and channel owner, including differences between customers, prospects and advisor-managed relationships.

Integration & events

Identify source events, latency and ownership across the website, app, CRM, data platform and advisor tools. Test duplicate handling, retries and exits before relying on them.

Regulatory & data controls

Great Eastern’s compliance, risk and security functions would need to review communications content, hosting, data residency, access, retention and AI governance against its requirements and applicable Singapore expectations. Ask Netcore for current compliance documentation for this review.

Measurement & migration

Agree a baseline, a control group and complete costs. Plan cutover so old and new journeys do not run in parallel, and decide which messages stay outside scope.
